Start with THORChain and Maya.
Keep provider choice open.

This rollout focuses on reliable access to blockchain data. Liquify remains the preferred partner; compatible independent providers can supply backups. Other projects can choose their own primary and backup providers.

Choose the service your application actually uses

The initial storefront includes registered THORChain/Maya mainnet services and Bitcoin/Ethereum RPC. This is a starting selection, not a complete inventory of every application dependency. A node RPC, a REST API and a Midgard indexer are different services and need compatible backups.

No chain is provisioned simply because it appears in the registry. Other registered services remain available through the service scope selector.

Keep your primary and add protection

Choose Liquify for the initial primary where it provides the exact required service. Add one or two independent providers for that same service, arrange usable contracts, then test failure and recovery. The subscriber retains your selected priority during health polling and discovery refreshes.

A registered online status is a provider declaration. It does not establish measured uptime, capacity or backup readiness.

Providers and consumers join through the existing flows

Providers register their own services and terms. Consumers select a service, compare providers and review the contract before signing. Liquify preference is a rollout setting; participation and consumer choice remain open.

The initial release needs an operator rehearsal with real compatible endpoints and funded backup contracts before community traffic can depend on it.
